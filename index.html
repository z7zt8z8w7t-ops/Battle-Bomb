BATTLE BOMB

Don’t be left holding the bomb when it explodes.

HOW TO PLAY

1. Everyone starts with 3 little bombs. Lose a round and one gets burnt out.

2. The highlighted player answers the prompt first.

3. After answering, tap the bomb to pass it to the next player. The first pass lights the fuse.

4. Keep answering and tapping the bomb to pass it on.

5. If the bomb explodes while you’re holding it, you lose a life.

6. Lose all 3 lives and you’re out. Last player standing wins.
